How It Works

One working cycle, three simple jobs.

1 · Follow the row

IR sensors track the guide line and the PID loop keeps the bot perfectly centred between the crops.

→

2 · Detect & avoid obstacles

The ultrasonic sensor scans ahead. If something crosses the safety threshold, the bot halts, reroutes or calls for help.

→

3 · Identify & pluck produce

The servo gripper arm extends, identifies ripe fruit (optionally via camera vision) and plucks it into the payload.
